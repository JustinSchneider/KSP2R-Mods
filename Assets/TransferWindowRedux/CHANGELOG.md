# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-20)

- Requires KSP2 Redux 26w42c or later.
- With [Flight Plan Redux](../FlightPlanRedux) installed, a Flight Plan button opens it on the same transfer.
- The destination menu is as wide as its longest name, instead of cutting names off.
- Stays idle in campaigns that don't include it (Redux campaign features).
- Named and described in the Mods screen and the campaign feature editor.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-14)

First release: a port of ABritInSpace's [Transfer Window](https://github.com/ABritInSpace/TransferCalculator-KSP2) to KSP2 Redux.

- Phase angle, time to the next window and its date, the window after that, transfer time and relative inclination for any planet or moon you can reach from where you are.
- Ejection Δv and angle, capture Δv (or aerobraking), and a Δv budget against your vessel.
- Creates the ejection maneuver node for you, and warps to the window or node.
- Returns from a moon to its planet, aimed for reentry or a parking orbit.
- Works from the launchpad, using a parking orbit for departure figures.
- A stock-style window: draggable, resizable, and remembers its position and size.
- Translated into German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
